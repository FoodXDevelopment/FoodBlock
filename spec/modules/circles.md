# Circles

A **circle** is people who own things together, and the rows by which some of them act for the owners: a business, a household, a savings group, a co-op. It is an `actor` of kind `circle`, written by its founder as an ordinary block; its id is that block's hash. **A circle never signs and holds no key.** Every block `as` it (`refs.as`) is signed by a person as her own word, and speaks for the circle's owners only where a row holds for it (Rows) or every owner signs it together (Every owner together). Ground: only persons act and only persons own (Mises, *Human Action*; Rothbard, *Man, Economy, and State*).

No text says a circle pays, holds, chooses or decides. A word that speaks for a circle is written as who signed it, by which row, and which of whose things it names.

## Owners

- **Parts.** The founding block may state `state.pack`, the circle's parts, in whole parts: an amount whose `unit` is `part`, whose `value` is a whole number of at least 1, and which has no `of` (`shape`). Its founder owns all of them first, and the founding block is her start. Parts move as any amount moves, by their owner's transfer and the receiver's yes. A fraction of a part is another kind, so a draw of one takes nothing (`unit`). `pack` never changes.
- **Whose the circle's things are.** What the record shows as the circle's belongs to the persons who own its parts, by the parts each owns. A part is a share of those things as the circle's rows shape them. The founder wrote the rows when every part was hers, and each later owner was handed no more than that.
- **Owners unsaid.** A circle that states no `pack` leaves its owners unsaid (`owners: { not_said: true }`). Its things are held together under its rows; no row of it counts parts (`shape`); the protocol names no shares and fills none in. It has no every-owner way (Every owner together).
- **Unissued parts.** Parts of a circle that are among its own things are **unissued**. They are no one's share and count in no row. A transfer `as` the circle of its own parts is a move like any other: it holds by a row whose `scope.what` names the circle, or by every owner together. A row names ids in `scope.what` (one id or a list); a block's `refs.what` must be within it. A row that names the circle, or any block signed after the founding block, comes by a version: no block names its own id.
- **A circle as owner.** Parts of a circle held by another circle are that circle's owners' and count by a block `as` it for which a row of its own holds. Circles that own each other are neither refused nor named by any reason: `circle_state`'s owners and `ways_in` show that their owners end in no person (`no_person`), and a stranger weighs that himself.
- **Every owner together.** What hands that together own every part persons own sign is theirs to do, whatever the rows say. "Every part persons own" is the `pack` where no part is unissued; otherwise the `pack` less the unissued parts that the circle's meter's receipt of the move states. A circle with unissued parts and no meter, or with no `pack`, has no such way.
- **Joining, leaving, a close.** Only a transfer that someone accepts ends owning. A holder may always end her own place in a row's `to` or under a grant: it only narrows. A close ends her hands and moves no part: her parts move only by her own transfers, such as a will. A circle is sold by selling its parts. No block dissolves a circle: once its things are handed on, it holds nothing.
- **A buyer of parts, and blocks sealed before he bought.** An audience that names a circle names the owners of its parts in the circle fold of that block's causal past. A block sealed before a buyer's accept of his parts does not name him; it reaches him only as its holders hand it on. What was sealed before he owned was not his.

## What a circle's word names

- **What it names.** A block that speaks for a circle names what the record shows as the circle's, and nothing else of anyone's. An owner who wants more behind it signs her own promise (a vouch: profiles/vouches.md). A cap ("only what the circle holds when this falls due") is a term of the deal, read only against those who signed it.
- **A circle's account word.** An account word signed `as` a circle counts for the circle where a row whose `scope.types` names `observe.account` holds for it, or by every owner together.
- **The listing.** A move of the circle that gives to an owner, to a hand, or to a tool or circle of theirs, and is not in the causal past of a word of the circle that stands and is not yet done, is listed beside that word. Nothing is refused for it, and no sum is run. It is a reading (`row_holds`, `listed`).

## Rows

- **A row.** A row is the owners' standing grant, stated on the founding block or a version: `{ parts, scope }` (holders of that many parts between them) or `{ to, needed, scope }` (that many of the ids `to` names, or of the holders of the grant it names). `scope` is a grant's scope, read as a grant's is, on the circle's line. Every number is whole and at least 1; never `all` and never a share. A block `as` the circle holds where one row holds for it, or by every owner together.
- **Head counts.** A row's `to` may name a grant `as` the circle; the row then counts the holders that grant names, as it stands. That grant changes only by a newer word signed `as` the circle by a row whose `scope.types` names `grant`, or by every owner together. No name carries power by itself.
- **Bounds.** A row may state a line's bounds (`each`, `limit`, `every`), read as a tool's are, on the circle's line. A move above a row's bounds holds by another row whose bounds it fits, or by every owner together. A row's `every` needs no `until`. A row's bound reads a block's amount in the bound's kind, as a grant's does: a transfer of goods `for` a deal with a price is read as that deal's total at the amount it moves, so a row of "up to £300 a deal" holds for a till's sale of a £3 loaf. A transfer of goods `for` no priced deal keeps its own kind, and is above a money bound.
- **A grant from a row.** A grant signed `as` a circle is no wider than the scope of the row that holds for it, as a child grant is no wider than its parent, on every field the row states. A field the row leaves out bounds nothing: the row is the owners' own word, and their silence names no limit. A grant wider than every row is a move no row holds for (One answer for a block with no row): it waits for hands that make it, such as every owner together.
- **Anyone who owns no part.** Anyone who owns no part acts for the owners only under a grant `as` the circle, or where a row's `to` names her (A row). Seating someone is that grant. Unseating her is its stop, or a newer word in its place, signed `as` the circle by a row whose `scope.types` names `grant`. A holder may always end her own place under it.
- **Hands.** A **hand** is a block made on its signer's parts, or on her place in a row's `to`: the move itself, and each counted block that names it in `refs.confirms`. A hand counts in a parts row by the parts she owns of the circle on her line read whole from her hand back to her start; in a `to` row, once, where `to` names her, or names a grant that names her and stands in the hand's causal past. A hand on unissued parts, and the hand of one the move gives to, count for nothing (`not_voice`). A move holds at a block, the move or a confirm of it, in whose causal past enough hands of one row count, counted in that one place. Each hand stands as her word would (her key's `wait`, `reach` and bounds). Until the move holds and its hands stand, it is waiting (`on: hands`).

  **A hand lasts while she holds what the row counts.** Its ends (blocks.md, The cut) are: for a parts row, every draw of hers on the circle's parts, from where she signs it, landed or not, and her close; for a `to` row, every end of her place (a newer word for the grant without her, its stop, her own end, her close). A hand whose end the completing block has seen adds nothing. A draw is no end of her hand for the move that accepts that very draw (the move, or a move it confirms, answers it): its parts are hers until the receiver's yes, and the one saying yes is that receiver. For the cut, a move's signer is all its hands together: no hand's own later block passes an end of her hand, and no signer of one of its hands, no tool of hers and no one under a grant she gave is its relier. So, with no clock, a gathered move counts from where an outside block is joined to it.
- **No one is both sides** (authority.md, No one is both sides of a word, read for a circle). A move gives to an owner when it gives anything to her, to a tool of hers, or to a circle in which her hand could count. It holds only by a row met without the hand of anyone it gives to, or by every owner together, or under a row or grant whose `scope.on` names a promise that names her as the one paid.
- **A payout by parts** is a pattern (patterns/circles.md).
- **One answer for a block with no row.** A block `as` a circle for which no row holds is waiting (`on: hands`). It acts on nothing of anyone's, its signer's included. It binds his name to its truth, and nothing of his. `no_role` names this answer. A draft `as` a circle has no hand yet: whether it binds anyone (the door's `binds`) is read as if it were signed now and its signer's hand counted, so it binds where her row would hold for it, or every owner would sign it, and, unless it is an accept, something of the circle's moves.
- **Versions.** A **version** is an `actor` of kind `circle` that `updates` the founding block or an earlier version and states its whole `state`; the circle's id stays the founding block's. It is a block `as` the circle of type `actor`. Rows whose `scope.types` names `actor` are read only from the founding block: a version that states others changes nothing by them. With no such row, only every owner together makes a version. The rows as they stand for a block are those of the version in its causal past that every other version there is in the causal past of; where two versions are rivals, those of the latest version both have in their past; with none, the founding block's.
- **A circle's clock.** The founding block may state `clocks`, a named clock (blocks.md, A named clock). Every hand and every block `as` the circle is read on it: an end of a hand, or of a place a row counts, is seen by a block where it is in that block's causal past or where the clock received it first. Every grant `as` the circle that names the clock in its `scope.clocks` is cut on it (blocks.md, The cut). It changes only by a version.
- **A circle's line.** The founding block may name `refs.meter`: the start of the one line on which every draw that speaks for the circle is counted, read as `refs.meter` is. With none, each measure the circle owns is drawn on along the line of the key that signed the circle's start for it (folds.md, What is left). What a hand holds there is held as the circle's, never hers. It moves to another hand's line only as a part passed (folds.md, What is left), and at the end of her place (Hands, its ends), what is still on her line is freed to the circle, and another hand's block `as` the circle continues her line (folds.md, A line goes on).

## Beside the rules

- **No group may make outside dealing hard.** No row binds a holder's dealing with others except as her own signed promise. A fine, a boycott or an expulsion for dealing outside is a term she signed, with a stake, or it is nothing.
- **Shared records.** A pot, a vote or an auction is a record many write to, under a schema named in `refs.under` (profiles/pools.md, patterns/ballots.md). A count of such a record moves nothing; a block `as` a circle holds only by a row's hands or every owner together.

## Invariants

20. A block `as` a circle speaks for its owners only where a row holds for it, counted in one place, every hand counting in that row and past each of its ends, and no hand of one the move gives to counting; or where hands that own every part persons own sign it.
