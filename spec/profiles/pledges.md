# Profile: pledges

Optional. A threshold pledge (an assurance contract): "I pay £20 toward plan X if pledges for it reach £500 by 1 March." Two base schemas; core checks the form and reads neither.

**Adds:** the base schemas `pledge` and `pledge_close` (`spec/schemas`), and the reading of a close.

- **A pledge** is an ask (promises.md, Sides) naming the schema `pledge` in `refs.schema`. `refs.to` is the pledger, who signs it; `refs.from` the organiser; `refs.what` the plan (a thing); `terms.price` her money, a flat price; `terms.condition` her threshold, `{ pledged: <money> }`; `terms.expires` her date. Any other term is refused (`undeclared_field`).
- **Nothing is filled in** (folds.md, Counts). No default threshold, date or organiser. A pledge with no `expires`, whose price is not flat money, or whose condition is not `{ pledged: <money> }`, is written wrong: no close binds it.
- **A close** is the organiser's `observe` naming the schema `pledge_close` in `refs.schema` and the pledges it counts in `refs.derives_from`. Its date is its own `at`; with none it binds nothing.
- **What a reader checks.** Start from the pledges the close names that are live and fit it: each names the `pledge` schema and the close's author in `refs.from` (a block that speaks for that side, authority.md, Who a block speaks for); has no stop or decline by either side in the close's causal past (a stop the close did not see changes nothing, as an accept beats a withdrawal it did not see: promises.md, Who may bind); and the close's `at` is no later than its `expires`. Then drop any pledge whose threshold is more than the total price of those left that name its plan, in its threshold's currency (another currency adds nothing; nothing is converted). Repeat until nothing more drops. The pledges left are bound by the close; the rest are not. Only bound money counts toward a threshold, so every reader holding the same blocks gets the same set, and a close below anyone's threshold binds nothing of theirs.
- **The organiser's close is her own word.** It binds her name to what it says and her side (the plan) of each pledge it binds, and nobody else. A close cannot be taken back (`pledge_close` says `undo: never`, so an autonomous system closes only on its principal's yes, authority.md). Several closes may name one pledge; its price is owed once.
- **After a close binds her.** She owes `terms.price` to the organiser and pays by a payment `for` her pledge (promises.md, Settling). Either side may still stop onward; what is owed is only what the deal names. Core reads a pledge as an unanswered ask, which binds no one; door `condition_holds` says null. The organiser may answer each bound pledge `accepted`, so the stage fold reads it as binding; that answer is her own word.
- **Disputes.** The schema names no referee; a plan names one in its own schema's grounds, or both sides by a `referee` grant (promises.md, Disputes).
- **Money, or one unit of goods.** A threshold counts money in one currency, or an amount in one unit ("200 kg by Friday" for a village's haul call). The pledger's price is then an amount, and a pledge adds only to pledges in its own unit, as another currency adds nothing. A heads count is another schema: refusals.md.
- **Protected windows.** *Yours if unpaid by 30 June, but not within nine days of her calving.* The condition shapes read "not by T" (`without`, promises.md, By a time), not "not during a window W", and a window that moves with an event is not one of them. What the lender can sign today is a refrain with a stake: *I do not take her within nine days of her calving; if I do, £X of mine becomes yours.* It binds the lender's name and stake, not the title.
- **A held stake names its release,** or the reader shows "release not named" (vouches.md).
- **Recurring pledges.** A pledge may carry `repeat`: *£2 a week for six months if the shop opens.* The threshold is read as for any pledge.
- **A sealed assurance.** *I will if 30 of these 40 sign*, each signature sealed to the convenor until the close, is a promise conditioned on a `signal` by the named signers (promises.md, Conditions). The convenor holds every opened signature: below the threshold nothing is shown and nothing binds, and at it all are read at once. A reader shown only the public headers finds the threshold unmet and learns no name.
- **A walkway funded by a threshold.** De Soto's ten at £200 and forty at £50 are two pledges to one plan, each with its own threshold; the organiser's close binds the set that reaches them.
- **Each pledger counts for herself.** A close counts signers, never groups: paying or counting per group makes groups split.

Trust the schemas by hash (`spec/schemas/README.md`).
