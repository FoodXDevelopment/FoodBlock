# Profile: pools

Optional. A mutual pool: twenty dairy farmers each pay £50 a year into a pot, and a member whose cow dies is paid £800 when the vet they all named signs that it happened (Mises, *Human Action*, pp. 108-109: pool only a class). One base schema, `pool`; the fold `pot` (folds.md, A pot) reads it.

**Adds:** the base schemas `pool`, `draw` and `bounded_change`, and the terms `ceiling`, `above`, `if_short`, `loads_dues`, which only the schema declares (no fold reads them).

- **The head** is a promise by the holder under the schema `pool`. The holder is a circle or a named holder (the head speaks for her). The schema's grounds name the confirmers, the vet or vets all the members named.
- **Pay-ins** are transfers `for` the head `to_owner` the holder, accepted by her. Each is the payer's own promise, never forced equal.
- **A payout** is the holder's transfer to the member, conditioned on a `signal` by the schema's confirmers naming the event (things.md, Transfers). A payout signed ahead holds its amount of the pot from where it is signed (folds.md, A pot); so a holder signs a payout when the signal is in, and the most a member is paid is the head promise, a cap on the holder's word. A signal by anyone else does not make it hold, and a condition naming anyone but the confirmers counts for nothing (`not_confirmed`).
- **It never counts past the pot.** Each payout is a draw on the pot (folds.md, What is left, A pot). With no `if_short` the schema states, a payout past what is left takes nothing: there is no default order.
- **What voids a payout** is the member's own conduct: each is a promise not to do that member signed (promises.md, A promise not to do), listed in the schema's grounds. The fold does not read them; a referee does.
- **A pool names its end and its ceiling.** `terms.until` and `terms.ceiling`, and `terms.above` for what happens above it (for example, back to payers in proportion to what each paid in). A new ceiling is a new schema hash, which each payer signs for their own pay-ins. A treasurer renaming a drawer moves nobody's money.
- **A member's rank and first loss.** A member's own promise to the holder, under the pool schema, may carry `rank` (her place in the payout order, with its own `premium`) and `first_loss` (a first part of any loss that stays hers). They are her signed terms. No default order exists, and a pool with none pays nothing past the pot.
- **Kept or held.** A pay-in `to_owner` the holder is hers, and only her own promise brings anything back. Money a member wants kept apart is a thing (a marked tin) handed `to_holder` the holder, still the member's, and no pay-in (things.md, Transfers). The transfer itself says which; nothing is labelled.
- **Pools priced by class.** Each member's premium is what she signs. A pool of unlike risks either prices by class or splits. Pay-ins and payouts price things anyone can count (cows insured, hives), never a member's own statement of what she can pay.
- **Whether a claim raises dues** (`loads_dues`) is signed at founding, `true` or `false`, and never a default. With neither said the card shows "dues: not said".
- **Rule changes within signed bounds.** A member's promise under the schema `bounded_change` says what may change (the dues), by how many (`change_needed`: a whole number of members, folds.md, Counts), up to what figure (`change_up_to`), and how long she has to leave first (`change_notice`). It binds her only inside what she signed (circles.md).
- **A pot every member can add up.** The pot is the signed dues less the signed payouts, a fold any member reads (folds.md, A pot). A holder's own count that disagrees with it is shown as "unseen", never as theft: only a signed transfer moves what is owned.
- **Locked pot.** Under a row, a promise not to do is signed `as` the circle, *no transfer of this pot to a member*, with a stake on `ruled` (promises.md, A promise not to do), and a transfer of the pot to one it names, conditioned on a close (`closed`, identity.md, A will). It binds only those who signed, each later member by their own signature when they join. Before a close the pot is the holder's, and after it the named one's, and no member's.
- **Leaders answer by their own word.** Each leader signs a promise not to do, with a stake on `ruled`. No liability falls on a leader by custom or by the office.
- **Netting a debt against a payout** happens only by the member's own signed term, as a set-off does (promises.md, More worked examples).
- **A susu.** A collector keeps a trader's daily deposit and hands back the lot at day 30, less a fee. It is one standing promise (promises.md, Terms: `repeat`); each deposit is a transfer naming its occurrence, and the collector's accept is her receipt. The fee is a flat price, or a `forfeit` kept only if she stops early, because her term says so. Each day's money is handed `to_owner` the collector when she may use it, and the head promise (`from` her, the price what comes back at day 30) is her debt; when she only keeps it, it is a marked tin handed `to_holder` her, and answers for it by her own word.
- **A market circle with a queen.** The circle's schema names its referee, the queen and the elders by their ids, and says how many of them must agree (`grounds.needed`, promises.md, Disputes). Each stallholder signs the schema by naming it, so the queen is a judge both chose. A pooled reserve held beforehand is a pot whose holder is the circle.
- **A supplier-paid loan** is promises like any other: the supplier signs a payment to the lender `for` the buyer's loan, the buyer signs to repay the supplier, and each signs only what is its own.
- **A rotating pot (an equb).** Each member signs a standing promise to the pot; the pot pays one member each round. The order is a **draw anyone can check** (the schema `draw`): before the close each member commits to a salt (privacy.md, A commitment); the order is the hash of all the reveals under a ranking recipe named by hash; the schema's `terms.reveal_by` is a time and `terms.recipe` an id, and `terms.wins` a whole number of at least 1, how many win (a draw that states no count is no draw: `shape`; the count is her signed word, the protocol fills in none) (`protocol.json`, `kind_fields`); a member who has not revealed by the deadline, on the named clock, takes the last turns, by her own signed term, and her silence moves no one else's place. Turns belong only to the members who signed the draw, by their ids, never to bare keys; one person with many keys is named in the threat model. **A swap of turns** needs the signatures of the two who swap and of anyone whose turn moves. **Set-off**, one payment repaying an advance and part of the pot, happens only by the payer's signed term, never by the collector's choice.
- **Pay per death with no treasury (an iddir).** Each member signs a standing promise: *when a member dies, as the named checkers confirm, X of mine becomes the family's.* The promise's `repeat` is an event repeat whose `by` names the checkers' ids (promises.md, Terms), so only the checkers' own `observe` starts an occurrence, and the family's word alone starts nothing.
- **A run rule.** A rule that leavers are cut at the cut (blocks.md, The cut) is the circle's own signed term. A leaver takes what is hers and is bound by nothing after it.
- **What is backed by force says so.** FoodBlock gives proof and a judge both sides chose, never force. An equb backed by cheques, courts and prison is backed by force, and its docs say so.
- **Share-outs are per signer, never per circle.** Paying per group makes groups split to collect more.
- **Sharing a loss after the fact.** A circle's signed rule: a loss of one member's goods on a shared carrier is shared among members by the value each had aboard, with a named confirmer of the event. Each member signs, beforehand, a promise to the holder whose price is a `fraction` of the loss figure the confirmer signs (promises.md, Terms: a price fixed later): half, three tenths, two tenths, adding to the loss. It binds only the members who signed.
- **A layered pool.** A bigger party stands behind the pot by a vouch (vouches.md), whose receiver is the pool's circle.

Trust the schemas by hash (`spec/schemas/README.md`).
