# readings/readback

Not normative. The answer a read-back renders (profiles/short-form.md, A read-back that can be redone): what a block says, read for one person, from typed fields and the engine's own ops only. Free text is quoted, never read; nothing is classified. Its cases are `readback.cases.json`: each gives the blocks held, the block, the person it is read to, and the answer.

## Inputs

`readback(block, held, to)`: the block, the blocks held (a list; anything in it that is not an object is dropped; the block is added if not held) and `to`, the id of the person it is read back to, or null. Below, *all* is the held blocks, each id once (its first place in `held`), then the block if it was not held. Every op named is the door op of that name, asked over *all*.

## Shapes

- **A person** `{ id, code, name }`: `code` is `short_code(id)`. `name` is null where `id` is `to` (she is "you": her own name is never read). Otherwise her *own name blocks* are the blocks of all of type `actor` whose `refs.author` and `refs.updates` are both `id`, whose `state.name` is text, and that are valid (`valid` empty). Their *heads* are those no other of them `sees`. With exactly one head, `name` is its `state.name` and the head and its pieces held (step 1's walk, nothing lacked) are read; otherwise `name` is null and every own name block and its pieces held are read. Of an id that is not text: null.
- **A thing** `{ id, code, name }`: `name` is the held block's `state.name` where it is a `thing` and that is text, else null; `pack` and `stated_by` (a person: its `refs.author`) are added where its `state.pack` is an amount.
- **An amount** is copied as signed: `{ value, unit }` or `{ value, unit, of }`. Amounts are compared as exact decimals. Its *kind* is the text `JSON.stringify([unit, of or null])`; kinds are sorted by that text.
- **`lacks`**: the ids, unique and ascending, then the entries that are objects, unique by canonical JSON and sorted by it.

## The answer

The *signer* of a block is its `refs.author`, or, for a genesis, its own id. **Read** starts as the block's id; **lacks** as empty.

1. **Pieces.** From the block, walk `refs.author` and `refs.key`, and from each piece held that has a `refs.author`, its own two: each held piece is read, each not held is lacked.
2. `viewer` is the person `to`; `for` is `{ id, code, type, signer }`, `signer` the person of the block's signer.
3. **Who said it.** Where `valid(block)` is not empty: if every code is `unmet`, the answer is `not_shown: true`; else `not_valid` is the list of codes. Nothing more is said.
4. **What it binds.** `read(block, all, null)`. Codes other than `names_missing` and `names_unread`: `not_read` is `{ fields, rule_set, codes }`, `fields` the number of `undeclared_field` codes, `rule_set` whether one is `unknown_rules`, `codes` the codes, unique and sorted; nothing more is said. A refusal's detail is an engine's own words and is never read. Where `names_missing` or `names_unread` is there, `needs(block, all)` is lacked. Each id of `needs(block)` that is held is read, with its pieces held.
5. **The deal.** A promise with `status` `accepted` that `answers` a held block is read on that block (read); one it answers that is not held is lacked and it is read on itself. Else the deal is the block. *Terms* are the deal's `state.terms` (an object, else none). The *giver* is the deal's `refs.from` for a promise, its signer for a transfer, else the block's signer. The *receiver* is `refs.to` for a promise, `refs.to_owner` (else `refs.to_holder`) for a transfer, `refs.to` for a grant. `to` in the answer is the person of the receiver.
6. `audience`: `{ readers: n }`, n the length of the block's `state.audience` where it is a list, else `"public"`.
7. **Her record.** The *other side* is, where the viewer is the block's signer, the side of the deal that is not the signer (for a promise, of `refs.from` and `refs.to`, the first that is text and is not the signer; else the receiver); otherwise the signer. Where it is text and not the viewer: the *joins* are the blocks of all, other than the block and the deal, that the viewer signs and that name the other side in any ref but `author`, or that the other side signs and that name the viewer so; each is read. `other` is the person of the other side (else null); `first_deal` is true where there is no join; `other_side` is `from` or `to`: which side of the deal the other side is (for a promise, `from` where it is `refs.from`; else `from` where it is the giver).
8. **Things.** Where the deal's `state.what` (its own words for what moves, such as "20 kg tomatoes") is text, `what` is `{ text, by }`: `text` copied as signed, every character, and `by` the person of the deal's signer. It is quoted, never read; otherwise there is no `what`. What a deal names by id is in its lines, below. The named ids: of a promise, each `what` of `terms.lines` where that is a list that is not empty, else its `refs.what`; of a transfer with no `status`, its `refs.what`; else none. For each that is text, a line `{ what, by, to }`, `what` the thing, `by` the giver, `to` the receiver:
   - not held: lacked, `draw: { not_shown: true, lacks: [id] }`;
   - held and naming no `refs.of` (a kind): `draw: null`, and `amount` the promise's `terms.quantity` (a transfer's `state.amount`) where that is an amount;
   - else `ownership(thing)`: where null, as not held; each id in its `read` is read, and each block of all that `answers` one of them; where `not_shown`, every block of all is read where it names no `read`, `draw: { not_shown: true, lacks }` and its lacks are lacked; else the draw is the one whose `block` is the block, else the deal's.
   - No draw: the line is given only where `read_whole(block, thing)` is whole (an error is not whole), as `draw: "not_drawn"` with `owners: [{ owner, amount }]` (each owner a person, `amount` or null).
   - A draw: `by` and `to` become its own; `draw` is `{ state }`, else `{ why }`, else `{ state: null }`; `holder` is the person of the draw block's `refs.to_holder` where that block is a transfer and its holder is not the draw's receiver, else null; `freer` is the person of the draw's receiver where its state is `held` (whose word frees it), else null.
   - **A pair.** The mates are every block in a pair of `fork_pairs(all)` with the draw's block. Where a draw of the ownership answer other than this one has a mate's block, `pair` is each such draw (this one too) as `{ draw: its block, to: person, yes }`, sorted by `draw`; `yes` is whether a block of all `answers` it with `status` `accepted`, signed by its block's `refs.to` or `refs.to_owner` (each such block is read); each draw's block is read.
9. **Amounts**, per side and per kind: of each side, the most of hers that goes out and the least that comes in, at both ends of every range. The *ends* of a value are its `min` and `max` where it has either, its `one_of` list, else the value. For a promise deal: where it names a thing or states `terms.quantity`, each end of the quantity that is an amount goes out from `refs.from` and in to `refs.to`; for each end of `terms.price` and each end of the quantity (or none), `terms_total({ price, step?, quantity? }, quantity or null)`, each total that is an amount going out from `refs.to` and in to `refs.from`; where there is a total, `money` is `[{ amount: the largest total, by: refs.to, to: refs.from, draw: null }]`. For a transfer deal with no `status`, an amount and no named thing: it goes out from the giver and in to the receiver, and `money` is that one. `amounts` is `[{ side, out, in }]`, sorted by side id, `out` and `in` sorted by kind.
10. `terms`: `{ waits_on }`, `terms.waits_on` where it is `price` or `goods`, else null.
11. **A grant** whose `state.kind` is not `key`: `worst_case(block)`. Where `not_shown`: `grant: { not_shown: true, lacks }`, its lacks lacked. Else each `on` promise is read, each `refs.what` held is read (else lacked), `refs.meter` is read where held, and `grant` is:
    - `holder` (person), `can_lose` (copied), `meter_own` (its `meter` is `own`), `pays` (its `pays` is true);
    - `pay_at_most`, `hand_over_at_most`: its `at_most`, split: a kind that is the kind of an end of the quantity of an `on` promise of side `from` is handed over, every other is paid;
    - `clocks`: where `scope.clocks.names` is a list, `{ hear, all, one }`: `hear` each name as `{ block: { id, code }, of: person of its author }` where held (read), else `{ block: null, of: person of the name }`; `needed` is `scope.clocks.needed` where a whole number, else the number of names; `all` is needed equal to the names, `one` is needed 1 of one name; else null;
    - `backed_by`: with one name, each promise of all `for` this grant, signed by that name's owner, whose `terms.backing` is there (read), as a person, unique;
    - `floor`: of each `on` promise of side `from` whose price is a range with a `min`, or a `one_of`, the end with the least `amount`, as `{ amount, per }`.
12. Every block read is read with its pieces held (step 1's walk, nothing lacked) and its `prev` held, and theirs, to the line's start. The answer ends with `read`, sorted ascending, and `lacks`. A second reader holding the ids of `read` (and the block) gets the same answer.
