# Pattern: circles

Not normative. A pattern adds no rule: it is a schema and a worked example over the core (circles.md), and no word reads it.

## A payout by parts

A standing promise to each owner, signed by every owner together, names how takings are paid to her by parts: when, the `step` her share rounds to, and, on one of them, that she takes what is left. Each payout is one transfer to one owner, `for` her promise, signed `as` the circle under a row whose `scope.on` names it; the row's `to` may name the till. A tool under a grant pays only what a deal names: a promise that states no amount names nothing it may pay (`not_due`).

**Worked example.** Amara and Leila own the café F, 50 parts each. They sign `as` F promises SP-A (to Amara) and SP-L (to Leila): "each Sunday, the till's takings by parts, step 1p; Leila takes what is left", and a version of F adding the row `{ to: [T], needed: 1, scope: { types: [transfer], on: [SP-A, SP-L] } }` (it names blocks signed after F, so it comes by a version). Sunday, takings £306.75: the till signs `as` F £153.37 to Amara `for` SP-A and £153.38 to Leila `for` SP-L. Both hold, by the till's place in that row; no owner signs on Sunday. Under a grant to the till, `scope.on` SP-A, the same payouts are `not_due`.

## Default tables

Ordinary people never write a row. A host offers one table whole, reads each row back in one sentence, and shows `ways_in`. No staff grant names `observe.account`. Every table offers `needed: 1` among living owners and names a meter. At founding each owner is offered a will for her parts, unticked.

- **The café.** F: `pack` 100 part, `clocks: { names: [N] }` (N the café node), `refs.meter` T (the till's key grant), and one row `{ parts: 1, scope: { types: [promise, transfer, transform, observe, thing, grant], each: £300 } }`; no row names `actor`. Read-back: "Café: you 50, Leila 50. Either of you signs up to £300 a deal and may hire inside that. More, or a rule change: both." At three owners add: "Any one other owner may pay you, and you her."
- **Title until paid.** A stranger selling to a circle on credit has his delivery drafted on the condition of his own `paid` signal, his to remove. His read-back: "Their word. Nothing is held for you. Amara and Leila may move everything out before Friday."
- **A job.** Drafted as "counted on Ben's word, disputed before the judge both named", shown to Ben before he signs.
- **The savings group.** `pack` 12, one part to each saver; `{ parts: 8, scope: { types: [promise, observe, grant] } }`; no `actor` row. In round k each saver pays saver k directly, her own transfer `for` the round promise. Nobody holds the pot.
- **The family.** `pack` 2; `{ parts: 1, scope: { types: [promise, observe, transform, thing, transfer], each: 3 head } }`.
- **The co-op.** `pack` 1000, unissued parts held by the co-op, meter the co-op node; a members grant M and head-count rows on M (circles.md, Rows), added by a version.
