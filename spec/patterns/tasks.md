# Pattern: tasks

Not normative. A pattern adds no rule. A task is a promise, a deed the worker's own block, evidence an observe. No new type or verb.

**Adds:** the condition schemas, the progress kind, skills as offers, and the horizon rule. Relative `when` is in promises.md.

- **The plan.** The top of a plan is a promise to oneself (promises.md). Each sub-task is its own first promise and names its parent in `refs.for`.
- **Dependencies are conditions.** A step that must follow another has `terms.condition` under a schema: `{ settled: <promise id> }` or `{ deed: <block id> }`. A step waiting on the world has a sensor condition (below) or a signal from named people (promises.md, Conditions; the count is folds.md, Counts).
- **Sensor conditions.** `terms.condition` under the schema `reading_condition`: `{ reading, kind, op, value, over }`. `reading` is the sensor thing; `kind` the `state.kind` of the observes to read; `op` one of `lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge`, `eq`; `value` an amount `{ value, unit }`; `over` a duration. The performer's edge checks the observes it holds that: have that `kind`, name the sensor in `refs.from`, are signed by someone who speaks for the sensor (authority.md, Who a block speaks for; attestations.md, Devices), carry an `amount` in the condition's unit and an `at`, and are in the causal past of the block it is read for. **The window** is that block's own `at` less `over` up to its `at` (her word, read against her); a block with no `at` holds nothing. Read by a rule set that runs it (a bare `reading` is a named reading the core runs none of: promises.md, Conditions), the condition holds when the reader has the latest reading at or before the window's start and it, and every reading inside the window, keeps to the rule. Where several readings share that latest instant, all must keep to it. With no reading at or before the start, it does not hold. A reading from anyone else is evidence, never grounds. A reading never settles a promise (promises.md, Settling).
- **Windows.** `state.when` may be a window: any of `not_before`, `not_after` (times) and `takes` (a duration, the performer's estimate, not a promise), at least one (blocks.md; `protocol.json`, `shapes.when_window`). The keys are checked on any block.
- **Skills are standing open offers.** A mower, drone or cook publishes a `promise` (offer): `refs.from` itself, `to` open, one line in `terms.lines` whose `what` is a job kind (a `thing` of kind `schema`, such as `job:spray`) and whose `price` is the rate, `terms.needs` (a place and radius under the job's schema), and a `repeat`, or `expires` for when the offer ends.
- **Certifications are confirms.** A certifier's `confirms` of that offer is the certificate; the schema's grounds name who may confirm.
- **Progress.** An `observe` of kind `progress`, `for` the ask, `state.phase` (a label from the job's schema), optionally `state.fraction` (a decimal string). Sign a change of phase, not a heartbeat; a fast stream stays at the edge and enters by Merkle root (things.md).
- **The horizon.** Advice: a planner sends an ask only for a step it can really release and keeps the rest as a draft on its own node.
- **The scheduler is an edge service** that holds the near horizon, reads skills, picks whom to ask, watches progress and replans, offline. For a shared thing on the site it orders only under the owner's grant; a backup takes over only by a new grant from the owner. A model controls a machine only under its owner's grant, and otherwise may only rank.
- **A machine is an actor of its own,** acting for its owner only under her grant (identity.md). It posts its own asks.
