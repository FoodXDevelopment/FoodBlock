# Code of conduct

FoodBlock is built in the open, by and for people who grow, make, sell and eat food. This page says how we treat each other here, and what content is not allowed on any node run by FOODX WORLD LIMITED.

## How we treat each other

This covers the repository, its issues and pull requests, and every space run for FoodBlock.

- Be kind, patient and direct. Criticise ideas, never people.
- No harassment: no insults, threats, slurs, unwanted sexual attention, stalking, or publishing someone's private information.
- No discrimination by age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender, nationality, race, religion, sexuality or wealth.
- No impersonation. Every block is signed; do not pretend to be someone else, in a block or anywhere else.
- No spam. An agent may draft a message to a stranger; a person sends it (`spec/modules/consent.md`).

## Content

Every node operator answers for what it hosts and follows the law where it runs. On nodes run by FOODX WORLD LIMITED:

- Illegal content is not allowed. This includes child sexual abuse material, which is removed at once and reported to the Internet Watch Foundation and the police.
- So are content that harasses, threatens or exploits anyone, fraud, and false safety or food claims made to cause harm.
- Users must be 16 or older (https://foodx.network/terms).

## Protecting yourself

The protocol gives every person the same tools, whatever their age:

- **Stop.** Sign a `stop` consent and nothing from that sender reaches you or binds you. Only you can lift it (`spec/modules/consent.md`).
- **Admission.** Your node takes in only what your admission rule allows, with a daily limit for strangers (`spec/modules/nodes.md`).
- **Answer.** Anyone a block is about can always see it, answer it, and sign a `defeats` block that disputes it.

## Reporting

Email hello@foodx.network. Say what happened, where, and include the block hash or link if there is one. We reply within 3 working days, and keep the name of whoever reports private.

## What happens

We read every report. Depending on what happened, we may warn someone, remove their content, refuse their blocks on our nodes, or ban them from the project's spaces. Illegal content is reported to the authorities.
