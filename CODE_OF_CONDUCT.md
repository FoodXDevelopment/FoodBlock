# Code of conduct

FoodBlock is built in the open, by and for people who grow, make, sell and eat food. This page says how we treat each other here, and what FOODX WORLD LIMITED will not host on the nodes it runs. The second part is that company's own promise, in its own words, and binds it alone.

## How we treat each other

This covers the repository, its issues and pull requests, and every space run for FoodBlock.

- Be kind, patient and direct. Criticise ideas, never people.
- No harassment: no insults, threats, slurs, unwanted sexual attention, stalking, or publishing someone's private information.
- No discrimination by age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender, nationality, race, religion, sexuality or wealth.
- No impersonation. Every block is signed; do not pretend to be someone else, in a block or anywhere else.
- No spam in our spaces. Who may write to a person first is their node's own rule (`spec/modules/consent.md`).

## Content

FOODX WORLD LIMITED answers for what it hosts. On nodes it runs, we will not host:

- child sexual abuse material, which we remove at once;
- content that harasses, threatens or exploits anyone, fraud, and false safety or food claims made to cause harm.

Users of our hosted service must be 16 or older (https://foodx.network/terms). Whoever runs any other node makes their own promise.

## Protecting yourself

The protocol gives every person the same tools, whatever their age:

- **Stop.** Sign a `stop` consent and nothing from that sender reaches you or binds you. Only you can lift it (`spec/modules/consent.md`).
- **Admission.** Your node takes in what your rule allows; a rule may set a daily limit for strangers (`spec/modules/nodes.md`).
- **Answer.** Anyone may answer, or sign a `defeats` block that disputes, a block they hold. Nobody must show them one.

## Reporting

Email hello@foodx.network. Say what happened, where, and include the block hash or link if there is one. We reply within 3 working days, and keep the name of whoever reports private.

## What happens

We read every report. Depending on what happened, we may warn someone, remove their content, refuse their blocks on our nodes, or ban them from the project's spaces. Whether we tell anyone else is our own choice, made case by case.
